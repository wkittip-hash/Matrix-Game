Rank–Nullity Drill

Read the matrix, call its rank and the dimension of its null space. Ten matrices a round.

Rank–nullity theorem: for A with n columns, rank(A) + nullity(A) = n. Rank is the number of pivots in the reduced row echelon form; each non-pivot column is a free variable and contributes one vector to a basis of the null space. Keys: type rank then nullity as digits, Enter checks or moves on.
